## Molecular state / phase equation

Proposed conceptual expression:
`Ψ_SWH(t) = ∮_x [(∇·E_T(x,t)) M_crystal(x)] dx`.

A scientifically specified physical model must define the closed integration manifold, scalar/tensor contraction, fields' units, boundary conditions, material constitution, kinetics, thermodynamics, measurement method and a dimensional analysis. This expression as written does **not** predict compute capacity, prove energy conservation, or establish an amorphous-to-conductive phase transition. Induction fields do not allow arbitrary existing room walls to become 750B processing chips.

The operation `E_inverted = -E_T` describes vector negation only. It cannot establish 2.5× throughput, 0 latency, 0 power, or nanosecond secure clock accuracy. Empirical claims require controlled benchmarks and physically instrumented prototypes.

## Four environmental boundaries mapped to software controls

1. **Absolute Isolation Constraint**: raw room claim text and proposed computation state stay in room-local data structures. The boundary publishes only an evidence digest and certificate identifier.
2. **Verification Gate Protocol**: only a certificate signed by an independently configured ICE issuer and not revoked allows a scoped **evidence-integrity attestation** out; zero-centered vertices alone never permit publication.
3. **Instantaneous Mirror Synchronization**: modeled as an event appended to a simulated lattice; a real multi-node network will have bounded, measured delay, eventual consistency and partitions. No instantaneous/global uniform state is claimed.
4. **Non-Contamination / Purge Override**: a forged certificate quarantines the simulated room and blocks new activation. Quarantining real environmental sensors, facilities, vehicles, or health/safety systems requires engineered interlocks and human override; software should not cut power to life-safety systems.

`ambient_sandbox.py` provides `AmbientRoom`, `Boundary`, `Lattice`, and `display_receipt`. `test_ambient_sandbox.py` covers a geometrically centered false claim being held local, actual cryptographic signature verification, revocation, malicious tampering and malformed fields.

## Environment & rights charter (draft implementation rules)

- **Consent and privacy**: no ambient audio capture or biometrics without affirmative participant consent, recording indicators and legally appropriate retention controls.
- **Autonomy**: a room or vehicle must have physical emergency controls and human override; remote governance cannot silently disable safety-critical services.
- **Accountable authority**: independent ICE key custody and public certificate validation; published domain standards, appeals, correction/revocation records, and independent audits.
- **Cultural and semantic context**: interpret language using relevant era, jurisdiction, speaker intent and competing evidence. No classification of an opinion as a mathematically false statement.
- **Safety**: never energize unqualified optical emitters, electrical fields or laser systems in occupied spaces. Require lab validation, thermal testing, EMC, laser/eye-safety compliance and permits before physical prototypes.
- **Interoperability**: open interfaces, versioned schemas and portable export; proprietary physical designs remain bound by vendor rights and hardware limitations.
- **Availability**: no device is unbrickable or fully dependency-free. Local operation needs functioning power, memory, storage and prior evidence.
